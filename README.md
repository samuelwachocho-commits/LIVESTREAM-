# CeremonyStream

Record and live-stream ceremonies, church services and events from your phone, with name bars, banners, tickers, timers, clocks, logos and music mixed into the stream.

## Install (Android)
1. Open the app link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the blue **Install app** button, or Chrome menu (⋮) → **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.
3. Open CeremonyStream from your home screen. Allow camera and microphone when asked.

## Notes
- Needs `https://` (GitHub Pages provides this) for camera access.
- Recording works with no server. Live streaming needs a streaming server: set its address in `index.html` (`window.CS_SERVER = 'https://your-server'`).
- Your overlays, logos and music are saved on your own phone only.
